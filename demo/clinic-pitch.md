# Clinic Pitch

## Simple positioning

**Your receptionist cannot answer every call. This system can handle routine appointment calls and after-hours enquiries, while your staff takes over whenever a human is actually needed.**

## What to demonstrate

Avoid leading with AI terminology.

Show:

1. patient asks for an appointment
2. system checks real slots
3. patient books
4. calendar updates
5. WhatsApp confirmation arrives

Then show one reschedule and one human-handoff example.

## Problems it can help reduce

- missed appointment enquiries
- repetitive availability calls
- manual rescheduling/cancellation work
- after-hours unanswered enquiries
- repeated questions about timings and fees

## What it does not replace

- clinical judgment
- treatment advice
- complex patient conversations
- staff escalation
- clinic policy decisions

## Pilot conversation

A practical first offer to a clinic can be framed as a limited pilot:

- one phone/voice entry point
- one calendar
- selected appointment types
- clinic FAQs
- human handoff
- WhatsApp confirmation

Prove reliability before expanding scope.
