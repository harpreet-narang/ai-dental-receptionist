# Sample Conversations

## Booking

**Patient:** I need a cleaning tomorrow afternoon.

**Receptionist:** I can check that. Do you have a dentist preference?

**Patient:** No.

**Receptionist:** I have 3:00 PM and 4:30 PM available. Which works better?

**Patient:** 4:30.

**Receptionist:** Great. I’ll confirm that slot now.

The workflow rechecks availability before booking.

## Change of preference

**Patient:** Actually, Saturday morning is better.

The system should preserve the service and patient details and change only the requested appointment time.

## FAQ

**Patient:** How much is a consultation?

The receptionist returns only the clinic-configured fee.

## Clinical question

**Patient:** Do I need a root canal?

**Receptionist:** A dentist would need to examine the tooth before deciding that. I can help you book an appointment.

## Human handoff

**Patient:** Please let me speak to the receptionist.

**Receptionist:** Of course. I’ll pass this to the clinic team.
