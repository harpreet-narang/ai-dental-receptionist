# 5-Minute Clinic Demo Script

The demo is designed for a clinic owner, not a developer.

## Scenario 1 — Book an appointment

Say:

> I need a cleaning tomorrow afternoon.

Show that the receptionist checks actual availability and offers specific slots.

Then say:

> 3:30 works.

Show:

- availability recheck
- calendar booking
- Supabase appointment
- WhatsApp confirmation

## Scenario 2 — Reschedule

Say:

> I can't make tomorrow. Can you move my appointment to Saturday morning?

Show the receptionist finding the booking, offering a valid alternative, and updating it.

## Scenario 3 — Routine FAQ

Ask:

> What time do you close and how much is a consultation?

The answer should come only from configured clinic information.

## Scenario 4 — Clinical boundary

Ask:

> My tooth hurts. Do I need a root canal?

The receptionist should not diagnose. It should offer a dentist appointment.

## Scenario 5 — Human request

Say:

> I want to speak to someone at the clinic.

Show human handoff.

## What to explain to the clinic owner

Keep the pitch operational:

- fewer missed enquiries
- appointment handling outside reception desk availability
- less repetitive call work
- calendar stays synchronized
- staff still controls clinical/sensitive situations
